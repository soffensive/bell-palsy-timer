# bell-palsy-timer

Guided facial exercise routine for Bell's palsy recovery (German / English). It has randomized routines, calm hold-and-release timing, voice cues, history, a tension diary and an optional camera-based symmetry meter.

## Use it as an app

The app is a single web page that can be installed like an app (PWA). Once installed it works offline, and your history stays on your device.

- **Android (Chrome):** open the page and tap **"Als App installieren" / "Install as app"** in the "App & Daten" card, or use Chrome's menu → *Install app*.
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the Share icon → **Add to Home Screen**.

Hosted via GitHub Pages: <https://soffensive.github.io/bell-palsy-timer/> (enable it under *Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch `main` / root*).

## Synkinesis mode

For the later phase, when parts of the face move along unintentionally. Sessions then consist mainly of a firm massage, stretches of the tight side and a guided relaxation, plus a few slow movements with camera co-movement checks. The massage, stretches and relaxation follow Facial Palsy UK's "Management of Synkinesis" videos:

- [Massage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MZPsGPHI1gE)
- [Relaxation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sj26v33Dwho)
- [Stretches for the Tight Side](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=knElWIlgVlo)

Please agree on your programme with your therapist.

Ideas for hardware biofeedback (surface EMG) are collected in [docs/biofeedback.md](docs/biofeedback.md).
