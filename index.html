Bell's Palsy Recovery Routine

9 exercises · about 10 minutes

Before you begin

Start once your doctor or therapist confirms that you're ready to exercise. For most people, this is often after finishing prescribed medication and once the acute phase has settled.

Go slowly, especially at first. Don't try hard, stop and rest if it hurts

  • Sit somewhere well-lit, facing a mirror or the built-in camera
  • Exercise twice a day, or follow your therapist's guidelines
